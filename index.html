Common Questions
  • You can book through Concur or UF GO, but it takes extra steps. Concur loads multiple Hilton rates, and the default is usually not your negotiated UF rate. If you book a different rate, Hilton's standard cancellation policy applies and you lose the flexibility that comes with your UF rate. Tax exemption and prepaid billing are also not applied automatically, and you would need to contact us separately with your confirmation number to set those up. Booking directly through us handles everything in one step: the right rate, tax-exempt, prepaid, and flexible cancellation; with no follow-up needed.
